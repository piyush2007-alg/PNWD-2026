Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your big day

How far in advance should I book your services?
We recommend booking at least 12–18 months in advance for premium dates. However, we also accommodate shorter timelines depending on availability. Contact us early to secure your preferred date.
What packages do you offer?
We offer three bespoke packages — Silver Elegance, Gold Prestige, and Platinum Royale. Each is fully customisable to match your vision and budget.
Do you handle destination weddings?
Absolutely! We have successfully planned luxurious destination weddings across Goa, Jaipur, Udaipur, Dubai, and Bali. Our team manages all logistics, travel, and on-site coordination.
Can we customise the décor and theme?
Yes — every wedding we plan is 100% unique. From colour palettes and floral arrangements to lighting and table settings, everything is curated exclusively around your personal style.
What is your cancellation policy?
We understand that plans can change. Our flexible cancellation policy varies by package and timeline. Full details are provided in your personalised contract upon booking.
