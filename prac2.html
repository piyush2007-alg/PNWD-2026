Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj

Founder of the Maratha Empire

About

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj was born on 19 February 1630 at Shivneri Fort. He was a brave warrior and the founder of the Maratha Empire in India.

He was known for his military skills, strong navy, and good administration. He was crowned as Chhatrapati in 1674 at Raigad Fort.
