👑 About Virat Kohli

Virat Kohli is one of the greatest cricketers of all time. Born on November 5, 1988, in Delhi, India, he is known for his passion, aggressive captaincy, and unmatched consistency. He has inspired millions of youngsters with his dedication and fitness.

🏏 Career Highlights

  • Debut in International Cricket: 2008
  • Former Captain of Indian Team (All Formats)
  • One of the best chasers in cricket history
  • Known as "Run Machine" & "King Kohli"

📅 Career Timeline

  • 2008: Made international debut
  • 2013: Became ODI vice-captain

  • 2018: Won ICC Cricketer of the Year
  • 2023: Continued breaking records

💪 Fitness & Personality

Virat Kohli revolutionized fitness standards in Indian cricket. His discipline, strict diet, and workout routine made him one of the fittest athletes in the world.
