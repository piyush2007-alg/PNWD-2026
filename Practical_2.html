Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj

Great Warrior and Founder of the Maratha Empire

Early Life

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj was born on 19 February 1630 at Shivneri Fort in Maharashtra.

His father was Shahaji Bhosale and his mother was Jijabai, who taught him bravery and good values.

Achievements

He captured many forts like Torna Fort at a young age.

He used guerrilla warfare (Ganimi Kava) tactics.

He built a strong navy to protect the coastline.

He was crowned as Chhatrapati in 1674 at Raigad Fort.

Legacy

Shivaji Maharaj is remembered as a symbol of courage, leadership, and justice.

He passed away on 3 April 1680, but his legacy continues to inspire people across India.
